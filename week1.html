Studio Above&Below

This is the website of the studio Above&Below. The studio is based in London and was founded by Daria Jelonek and Perry-James. They explore the connections between human, machines and the environment through mixed reality experience with digital art and data.

One of the works that stands out to me is their project (s)low-tech AI. The project responds to physical interaction with a rock by generating sound and images through a simple AI algorithm. The way they connect AI with nature and human interaction fascinates me. Using a rock as a medium creates a sense of wonder and makes the experience feel meditative and reflective. I especially like how the project presents AI as something slow and sensory, rather than simply as a fast and efficient tool.
