Studio Above&Below

This is the website of the studio Above&Below. The studio is based in London and was founded by Daria Jelonek and Perry-James. They explore the connections between humans, machines, and the environment through mixed reality experiences with digital art and data.

One project that stands out to me is (S)low-Tech AI. The installation uses rocks as a physical interface for an AI system that responds with sound and imagery.

What I find most interesting is how the project connects physical materials with digital interaction. Touching and rearranging rocks becomes a way of interacting with AI, which makes me think about physical materials from a different perspective. I also like how the project presents AI as something slow and reflective, rather than simply as a fast and efficient tool.
